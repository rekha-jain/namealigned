Lucky Days, Colours and Gemstones for All Nine Numbers

Every Chaldean lucky attribute in one place: days, dates, colours to wear and avoid, stones, metals, directions and months, side by side.
Reviewed by the NameAligned team · Last updated 29 August 2026 · methodology · sources

Lucky attributes are not magic, they are tilt. Chaldean tradition assigns every Birth Number a ruling planet, and that planet makes certain days, colours, stones, metals and directions more accessible to you than others. Cheiro's tables have been used by practitioners for over a century, and the useful way to read them is as a thumb on the scale rather than a law of physics.

The attribute that applies to you is set by your Birth Number, also called Moolank: the single digit your day of birth reduces to. Born on the 23rd? 2 + 3 = 5, so you read Number 5. Born on the 8th, 17th or 26th? You read Number 8. Everything on this page is organised that way.

Every number at a glance

This is the comparison most numerology sites never give you, because it makes the overlaps visible. Notice that Numbers 3, 6 and 9 share the same lucky numbers, and that Sunday appears as a strong day for four different numbers for quite different reasons.

How to actually use this

The attributes are not equally worth your attention, and treating them as equal is the most common mistake. Ranked by leverage:

  • Day and date come first. They are free, and they are the only attribute you fully control. Moving a signing, launch, registration or difficult conversation onto your ruling planet's weekday is the highest-value change on this page.
  • Colour is second. Cheap, low-risk, and it compounds quietly. It matters most where the stakes and the visibility are both high: weddings, pitches, interviews, launches.
  • Direction and metal are third. Worth setting once, then forgetting. Position the desk, buy the one ring.
  • Gemstones come last, despite being what most people ask about first. They are the most expensive and the most cautioned attribute in the entire tradition.

The gemstone caution, stated once properly

  • Traditional practice treats blue sapphire (Number 8) and cat's eye (Number 7) as strong enough to require a trial: wear casually for 7 to 10 days first.
  • If anything in daily life noticeably destabilises during the trial, remove the stone. That instruction is part of the tradition, not a disclaimer bolted on.
  • Stones interact with each chart differently, which is exactly why tradition recommends a practitioner for the high-impact ones rather than a chart from a website.
  • No stone is required. Every other attribute on this page works without one.

For high-stakes events, practitioners stack attributes rather than relying on one. A lucky date that also falls on your lucky weekday is meaningfully stronger than either alone, and that combination is what people are usually looking for when they ask about muhurat for a marriage, a registration or a business launch.

☀Number 1 · Sun

For those born on the 1st, 10th, 19th or 28th. Number 1 draws vitality from the Sun, and the luckiest hours fall between sunrise and noon while the Sun is still climbing. Decisions, signatures and openings made in that window land more cleanly than the same act attempted at dusk. The Sun rewards being seen, so this is the one number whose attributes work best when they are visible rather than discreet: the gold worn where people notice it, the announcement made publicly.

Why some numbers overlap

The repetitions in the table are not sloppiness in the tradition, they are structure. Numbers 3, 6 and 9 share the lucky set 3, 6, 9 because Jupiter, Venus and Mars form one of Cheiro's planetary triangles. Numbers 1 and 3 both take gold because Sun and Jupiter are traditionally allied. Numbers 2 and 7 share pearl and moonstone because Moon and Ketu are both reflective rather than radiant bodies. Where you see overlap, there is usually a stated planetary relationship behind it, and the compound numbers guide covers how those relationships were derived.

One honest note

None of these attributes are deterministic. A perfect lucky-day, lucky-colour, lucky-gemstone combination will not save a fundamentally misaligned decision, and none of them are required for a good life. They are tilt, not truth. Use them where the tilt is cheap and the stakes are high; ignore them everywhere else. Anyone telling you that a stone is mandatory, or that a wrong colour will cause harm, has left the tradition and entered the sales pitch.

Frequently asked

How do I know which number's lucky attributes apply to me?

Use your Birth Number (Moolank), the single digit your day of birth reduces to. Born on the 23rd? 2+3 = 5, so Number 5 attributes apply. The table on this page is organised by Birth Number.

Which is the most important lucky attribute to actually use?

Day and date, because they cost nothing and are easy to control. Choosing your ruling planet's weekday for a signing, launch or important conversation is the highest-leverage change. Colours are second. Gemstones are the most expensive and the most cautioned.

Do I need to wear a gemstone for my number to work?

No. Gemstones are optional and traditionally the most cautioned of all the attributes. Blue sapphire for Number 8 and cat's eye for Number 7 are strong enough that Chaldean practitioners recommend a 7 to 10 day trial before sustained wear.

Why do some numbers share the same lucky colours and metals?

Because the planets behind them sit in harmonic relationship under Cheiro's planetary triangles. Numbers 3, 6 and 9 share a triangle, which is why their lucky numbers overlap. Numbers 1 and 3 both take gold as metal because Sun and Jupiter are traditionally allied.

What if my lucky colour is one I dislike or cannot wear?

Use it in small or hidden ways: a lining, a pen, a phone case, a notebook, a file cover for the specific occasion. The tradition treats these attributes as tilt rather than requirement, so partial use is normal and there is no penalty for skipping one.
