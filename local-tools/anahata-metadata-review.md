## Fixes, in priority order

### 1. Add tags to the 9 videos that have none
These have no tags at all: `ow554pr8esE`, `ywUCSdLwD6I`, `K4_jZvdNnHk`, `_ofQTR2vhw8`, `XRk_QpJ6ZoI`, `ttOWBB-69LA`, `De_EJcVCAkE`, `7HjxYnjZ-dg`, `Vk-4nvfavhA`.
Tags carry little weight, but they help with misspellings and instrument names. Use 8 to 12 per video: instrument (bansuri, sarangi, sitar, tanpura, santoor), raga name if any, use case (sleep music, focus music), and 432hz.

### 2. Front-load titles to about 60 characters
Search and suggested video cards cut titles at around 60 to 70 characters. Several titles run past 90, so the instrument or use case gets cut off. Keep one emoji at most, and put it at the end.

### 3. Stop reusing "Enter Deep Flow"
Three videos use nearly the same phrase (`WyKnsPkn-Ss`, `Q6W42qi9fTA`, `GetYIeUvgF8`). They compete with each other for the same search. Give each one a distinct angle.

### 4. Make the first two lines of each description do the work
Only about 150 characters show before "more". Right now most descriptions open with mood-setting prose. Lead with the plain search phrase instead, then the link:

```
1 hour of slow sarangi music for deep sleep, tuned to 432Hz with delta waves. No vocals, no ads mid-track.
Find a track for your birth number: https://www.namealigned.com/meditation-music
```

### 5. Soften the "instant" health claims
"Lower Cortisol Instantly" and "Fall Asleep in Minutes" are the kind of medical-outcome claims YouTube's policies and advertisers react badly to. "For lowering stress" or "to help you fall asleep" carries the same search intent with less risk.

### 6. Build playlists that match the website sections
Create four playlists: Sleep, Anxiety & Emotional Calm, Focus & Study, Morning Ragas. Playlists rank in search on their own, and they lengthen sessions, which is the strongest legitimate growth signal for long-form ambient music.
