# Derived verdicts for the 16 previously-missing compatibility pairs

**Status: needs review.** These 16 verdicts were derived, not sourced. The 29
pre-existing compatibility pages were treated as authoritative and were not
changed. This file records the reasoning so each call can be checked or
overridden.

## The model used

Two inputs, in this order of precedence:

1. **The 29 existing verdicts**, which are treated as ground truth.
2. **Cheiro's number families**, which 26 of those 29 already follow:
   - Family A: **1, 2, 4, 7** (Sun, Moon, Rahu, Ketu) — strong with each other
   - Family B: **3, 6, 9** (Jupiter, Venus, Mars) — strong with each other
   - **5** (Mercury) — supportive with nearly everyone, the adaptable number
   - **8** (Saturn) — stands apart, pairs only with 4, cautioned elsewhere

Two documented exceptions in the existing data shaped the derivation:

- `4-5 = Caution` shows 5 is *not* universally supportive; it clashes with the
  4/8 karmic pair. This is why `5-8` is derived as Caution rather than Supportive.
- `1-9 = Strong` crosses families, reflecting the traditional Sun–Mars
  friendship. Number 1 is broadly compatible in a way 2, 4 and 7 are not, which
  is why `2-9` and `4-9` are Caution while `1-9` is Strong.

## Known conflicts with the deleted `number-N-in-love` data

The old per-number in-love pages carried their own compatibility lists, which
contradicted each other in 12 places and were therefore not used as a source.
Where they disagree with the derivations above, the specific conflicts are:

- **1-7**: page 1 said caution, page 7 said strong. Derived **Strong**, siding
  with page 7 and the family model.
- **2-4**: page 2 said supportive, page 4 said strong. Derived **Strong**.
- **4-7**: both said supportive. Derived **Strong** on family grounds. *Most
  likely of the three to warrant overriding back to Supportive.*
- **5-9**: page 5 said strong, page 9 said supportive. Derived **Supportive**.
- **7-9**: page 7 said supportive, page 9 said caution. Derived **Supportive**.
- **6-7**: both said caution/awareness. Derived **Supportive** on Venus–Ketu
  grounds. *Worth a second look.*
- **2-3**, **2-5**: both pages said awareness. Derived **Supportive**, since no
  traditional affliction exists between these benefics and 5 is the adaptable
  number. *Worth a second look.*

The three flagged above (`4-7`, `6-7`, `2-3`/`2-5`) are the weakest calls.
Everything else is either corroborated by the existing 29 or follows directly
from the family model.
