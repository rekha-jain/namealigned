# NameAligned Backlink Campaign Plan

Goal: earn relevant links to a useful public asset, not manufacture low-quality links. The first asset is the Chaldean letter values chart and embeddable name-number widget:

- Asset URL: `https://www.namealigned.com/resources/chaldean-letter-values`
- Supporting page: `https://www.namealigned.com/name-numerology-calculator`
- Widget script: `https://www.namealigned.com/assets/name-number-widget.js`

## What We Are Placing

1. Citation link to the Chaldean letter values chart from articles that explain name numerology, baby naming, lucky names, business names, astrology, or spiritual self-discovery.
2. Embedded mini calculator on blogs where an interactive tool genuinely improves the article.
3. Contextual mention of the free calculator where the article already discusses calculating a name number.

## 30-Day Execution Plan

### Week 1: Build the Prospect List

- Find 60-80 relevant prospects, not generic directories.
- Prioritize active blogs with visible authors, recent posts, and existing articles about names, numerology, astrology, baby names, relationship compatibility, branding, or spiritual wellness.
- Add prospects to `docs/backlinks/backlink-prospects.csv`.
- Record the exact article URL and a one-sentence reason the NameAligned asset improves that article.

### Week 2: Personalized Outreach

- Send 8-12 highly personalized pitches per day.
- Use one asset per pitch. Do not ask for multiple links.
- Prefer helpful angles:
  - “Your article explains name numbers but does not include a Chaldean values chart.”
  - “Your readers may benefit from an embedded calculator.”
  - “This section mentions lucky names; here is a practical way to verify the name number.”
- Track status, date sent, follow-up date, and response.

### Week 3: Follow-Ups and Partnerships

- Send one polite follow-up after 5-7 days.
- Offer a short custom paragraph, quote, or calculation example for their article.
- For bloggers who reply positively, suggest the widget only if it fits the page.
- Save every live placement URL.

### Week 4: Refresh and Expand

- Review placements and Search Console changes.
- Identify which asset angle worked best.
- Create one additional support asset only if outreach reveals repeated demand, such as “baby name numerology examples” or “business name numerology examples.” Do not mass-produce pages.
- Update internal links from relevant pages to the successful resource.

## Priority Prospect Categories

- Baby name blogs with articles about lucky names, numerology names, Sanskrit names, modern Indian names, or name meanings.
- Astrology and numerology blogs that explain Chaldean, Pythagorean, life path numbers, destiny numbers, or name correction.
- Spiritual wellness sites writing about self-knowledge, identity, archetypes, relationships, or emotional patterns.
- Branding and business naming sites that discuss brand name testing, founder names, numerology for business, or naming psychology.
- Relationship compatibility blogs where a lightweight numerology calculator can deepen engagement.
- Education/resource pages that list numerology tools or metaphysical calculators.

## Outreach Rules

- No paid link schemes.
- No fake guest posts.
- No mass blog comments.
- No irrelevant directories.
- No AI-spun guest posts.
- No exact-match anchor stuffing.
- No promises of ranking, traffic, or reciprocal links.
- Keep anchors natural: “Chaldean letter values chart,” “free name number calculator,” “NameAligned’s calculator,” “this Chaldean chart,” or a sentence-level citation.

## Tracking Metrics

- Prospects researched.
- Emails sent.
- Replies received.
- Live links earned.
- Widget embeds earned.
- Asset impressions in Search Console.
- Referring domains in GSC or external backlink tools.
- Clicks and impressions for `/resources/chaldean-letter-values`.
- Assisted impressions for `/name-numerology-calculator`.

## Weekly Review Questions

- Which topic category responds best?
- Which pitch angle receives replies?
- Are earned links going to the resource page or calculator page?
- Are placements relevant and likely to send qualified users?
- Does the asset need clearer examples, a downloadable chart, or a stronger embed preview?
