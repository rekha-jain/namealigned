Birthday Number 24 · Love, Money and Creative Power

Harmony · Abundance · Care

Born on the 24th of any month? Your Chaldean Birth Number is 6, ruled by Venus, the planet of love, beauty and comfort. In Indian tradition this day-based number is called the Moolank. The 24th reaches 6 through the compound 24, which the Chaldean tradition links with love, money and creative power, and with gaining support through partnerships and the goodwill of others. Your Life Path Number is separate: it comes from your entire birth date and describes your long-term direction, while the birthday number describes your natural, everyday style.

What does it mean to be born on the 24th?

24 joins 2, the Moon's emotional intelligence, and 4, Rahu's practical structure, and they add to 6, Venus. That combination is unusually grounded for a Venus number. The 24th-born loves beauty, comfort and harmony, but also knows how to build a home, a budget and a reliable routine around them.

The compound 24 is one of the warmer numbers in Cheiro's list, associated with help from people of influence, success through relationships and a talent for attracting both affection and resources. In plain terms: the 24th-born tends to do well when they work with others, and their generosity usually comes back to them. Compare the 6th and the 15th, the other Venus birthdays.

Personality strengths of the 24th-born

  • Warm, nurturing and loyal to family and friends
  • Strong aesthetic sense in homes, clothes and design
  • Practical about money, often a good saver
  • Creates harmony in groups and workplaces
  • Responsible, the person others rely on in a crisis

Growth themes for Birthday Number 24

  • Taking responsibility for everyone's happiness
  • Avoiding necessary conflict to keep the peace
  • Attachment to comfort that makes change feel hard
  • Expecting gratitude for help that was not requested

Venus thrives on balance. The 24th-born grows most when they give from choice rather than obligation, and let others carry their share.

The 24th in everyday life

The 24th-born usually has a beautiful, welcoming home, remembers birthdays and hosts the family gatherings. They enjoy food, music, gardens and good design, and often have a gift for making ordinary spaces feel special. Their practical 4 means they also handle the logistics behind the warmth, from the guest list to the household accounts. Many find deep satisfaction in long-term projects such as renovating a house or raising a close family.

How the 24th differs from the 6th and 15th

The 6th is pure Venus, gentle and home-loving. The 15th passes through a magnetic, charismatic compound and tends to draw attention. The 24th is the most practical Venus birthday, because its 4 gives structure to its warmth. That is why the 24th-born often turns creative taste into lasting financial security, through a business, a property or a well-run household.

Love and compatibility for the 24th

Venus is friendly with 3, 4, 5, 6 and 9 in the Chaldean table. Love is central for the 24th-born, who is devoted, romantic and committed. Jupiter-ruled 3 brings fun and optimism to Venus's warmth, making Number 3 and 6 compatibility a well-matched pairing. For a full couple reading, visit love compatibility numerology.

Career leanings

Interior design, fashion, hospitality, the arts, music, beauty and wellness, teaching, healthcare, family business, real estate and financial planning. The 24th's compound especially favours partnerships and client-facing roles. See Number 6 career paths.

Lucky attributes for the 24th

The 24th follows Venus: Friday, then Tuesday; pink, light blue, white and pastel green; diamond, white sapphire or opal; and the dates 6, 15 and 24, with late afternoon into early evening as the favoured window.
