Birthday Number 21 · The Crown of the Magi Under Jupiter

Achievement · Expression · Expansion

If you were born on the 21st of any month, your Chaldean Birth Number is 3, ruled by Jupiter, the planet of wisdom and growth. In Indian tradition this is your Moolank, the root number drawn from the day of birth. The 21st passes through one of Cheiro's most celebrated compounds, 21, the Crown of the Magi, associated with advancement, honours and success that arrives after sustained effort. What does it mean to be born on the 21st?

21 brings together 2, the Moon's receptivity, and 1, the Sun's initiative, and adds them to 3, Jupiter. That gives the 21st a rare mix: emotionally perceptive, yet ambitious; sociable, yet capable of real independence. Jupiter expands whatever it touches, so the 21st-born tends to grow into larger roles over time.

Cheiro described 21 as the Crown of the Magi, a symbol of elevation and victory after a period of effort. The pattern many 21st-born people recognise is an early stretch of hard work followed by a clear step up. Compare the 3rd, the 12th and the 30th, which share the Jupiter root.

Personality strengths of the 21st-born

  • Expressive speaker and writer, often witty
  • Combines charm with genuine ambition
  • Optimistic, with a knack for seeing the next opportunity
  • Generous teacher and encourager of others
  • Socially skilled, builds wide networks easily
  • Keeps going until the goal is reached

Growth themes for Birthday Number 21

  • Spreading energy across too many projects at once
  • Seeking approval before trusting their own ideas
  • Overpromising out of enthusiasm
  • Restlessness in the slow middle stretch of a goal

Jupiter grows best with focus. Choosing one main project per season and finishing it is the habit that turns 21's talent into the crown its compound promises.

How the 21st differs from other Jupiter birthdays

The 3rd, 12th, 21st and 30th all share Jupiter, but the compound changes the flavour. The 3rd is pure Jupiter, open and expressive. The 12th passes through a compound about boundaries and giving. The 30th leans reflective and thoughtful. The 21st is the most success-oriented of the group: its 2 gives social intelligence and its 1 gives drive, so it is the Jupiter birthday most likely to want recognition and leadership as well as knowledge.

In everyday life the 21st-born is often the organiser of gatherings, the person who connects friends who should meet, and the colleague whose presentations people remember. They thrive on variety, learning and conversation, and they tend to grow more confident and more successful with each decade.

Love and compatibility for the 21st

Jupiter is friendly with 1, 3, 6 and 9 in the Chaldean table. The 21st-born is affectionate, fun and loyal, and wants a partner who is also a good conversationalist. Venus-ruled 6 shares a love of beauty and comfort, making Number 3 and 6 compatibility a warm match. More on love compatibility numerology.

Career leanings

Teaching, publishing, media, marketing, law, consulting, coaching, ministry, hospitality and creative direction all suit 21. Its compound also favours roles that come with titles and recognition, such as senior management or public office. See Number 3 career paths.

Lucky attributes for the 21st

The 21st follows Jupiter: Thursday, then Friday; yellow, cream, violet and light blue; yellow sapphire, topaz or citrine; and the dates 3, 12, 21 and 30, with mid-morning as the favoured hour.
