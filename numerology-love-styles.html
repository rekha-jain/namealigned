How Each of the Nine Numbers Loves

The green flags, the shadow pattern, who each number reaches for, and how the relationship ages, for all nine Birth Numbers.
Reviewed by the NameAligned team · Last updated 29 August 2026 · methodology · sources

Chaldean numerology does not rank people as better or worse at love. It says each Birth Number loves in a different currency. Number 6 pays in daily acts, Number 5 pays in conversation, Number 8 pays in built structure, Number 9 pays in heat. Most relationship trouble between two decent people is a currency mismatch: both are paying, neither recognises the payment.

Your style is set by your Birth Number (Moolank), the single digit your day of birth reduces to. Born on the 23rd? 2 + 3 = 5, so read Number 5. Each section below gives the shape of the love, who that number reaches for, the green flags worth recognising, the shadow, and how it ages over decades.

Two of these categories deserve explaining, because they are not simply points on a good-to-bad scale.

Mirror is what happens when both partners share a number. These pairings are strong on recognition and weak on correction: you understand each other almost immediately, and you share the same blind spot, which means the shadow described in your section is running in both of you at once with nobody positioned to name it. That is a genuinely different situation from "compatible", and it is why same-number couples often report feeling deeply understood and strangely stuck at the same time.

Heavy applies to one pairing only, 4 and 8, the Rahu–Saturn combination Cheiro wrote about more than any other. It is not a caution and not a blessing. It is the pairing tradition treats as consequential: things happen faster and land harder, for better and worse. The 4 and 8 question covers why it earned its own category.

The broader shape behind the table is planetary. Numbers 3, 6 and 9 (Jupiter, Venus, Mars) are strong with each other across the board, the cleanest family in the system. Numbers 1, 2, 4 and 7 (Sun, Moon, Rahu, Ketu) form the other family and are strong with each other throughout. Number 1 (Sun) is the one number that reaches outside its family, strong with 9 as well and supportive with most of the rest, which fits a planet tradition treats as broadly compatible but easily overshadowed. Number 8 (Saturn) draws the most cautions, and Number 5 (Mercury) is supportive with nearly everyone, exactly what you would expect of the planet of exchange.

What compatibility actually means here

Compatibility in Chaldean numerology is not destiny. It describes the natural inclination: who fits without friction, and who fits with conscious work. Many lifelong marriages exist in pairings traditionally cautioned against, and the variable is almost always awareness rather than luck. A caution pairing is not a warning to leave; it means the friction is predictable, which makes it easier to work with than friction that keeps surprising you.

The shadow described in each section below is the cost of the strength, not a flaw to fix. Number 1's controlling streak is the same trait as its reliability. Number 6's peace-keeping is the same trait as its devotion. Naming it is not an accusation; it is information that helps a relationship survive its own pattern.

☀Number 1 · Sun · loves slowly, stays hard

Number 1 in love is steady, protective, and slow to commit but immovable once committed. The Sun does not flirt. When Number 1 chooses you, they usually do not change their mind, even when life makes the choice harder.

Number 1 is drawn to people who already have a centre: your own work, your own people, your own opinions. They do not want to be your only world; they want a partner with their own gravity.

Green flags from a Number 1 partner

  • Plans and follows through on plans
  • Shows up for the unglamorous days, not just the celebrations
  • Tells you the hard truth gently
  • Treats your career ambition as a shared project, not competition

The shadow

Number 1 can confuse leadership with control. It shows up when they start "improving" you, your habits, your friends, your work, instead of trusting your judgement.

Over the years

With a settled Number 1, long-term love feels like a fortress. The cost is that warmth has to be deliberately practised, because the Sun is not naturally tender, but it is loyal at a depth most people underestimate.

☾Number 2 · Moon · loves through tone, not words

Number 2 in love is emotionally porous, intuitive about your mood before you have named it, and almost embarrassingly devoted once they feel safe. The Moon loves through felt presence, not declarations.

Number 2 is drawn to people who can hold emotion without flinching: partners who do not run from sadness, who can sit with complexity, who can be patient with a heart that needs time to open.

Green flags from a Number 2 partner

  • Texts back even when the conversation has paused for hours
  • Notices small things, your favourite mug, the way you tense before phone calls
  • Does not punish you for emotional honesty
  • Creates routines, weekly dinner, Sunday walk, that anchor the relationship

The shadow

Number 2 can over-absorb a partner's feelings until they lose track of their own. It looks like resentment that arrives late, after months of unspoken accommodation.

Over the years

With a Number 2, long-term love is a slow-built sanctuary. The Moon's gift is that the relationship deepens with time; the early intensity matures into a quiet, reliable closeness most people never reach.

♃Number 3 · Jupiter · loves out loud

Number 3 in love is generous, expressive, and openly affectionate. Jupiter does not under-state. When Number 3 loves you, you and likely several other people will hear about it.

Number 3 is drawn to partners who can match their range: conversation, social life, intellectual play, travel. They get bored with sameness and thrive with someone whose world keeps expanding.

Green flags from a Number 3 partner

  • Introduces you to their world, friends, family, work, quickly
  • Plans experiences, trips, dinners, dates that feel like events
  • Speaks openly about the relationship, even to strangers
  • Encourages your ambition, even when it pulls you away from them

The shadow

Number 3 can confuse charm with depth. It shows up when the love stays in the warm-but-public layer and never goes into the harder private conversations the relationship needs.

Over the years

With a Number 3, long-term love means committing to keep growing together. Jupiter rewards expansion and stasis suffocates it, so couples who plan rituals of newness, learning, travel, shared projects, last longest.

◈Number 4 · Rahu · the unconventional heart

Number 4 in love is unconventional, intensely loyal, and not easily understood. Rahu does not love the way scripts say to love; it builds something that does not have a name yet.

Number 4 is drawn to partners who do not require them to be normal. They want someone who finds their oddness interesting, not something to manage.

Green flags from a Number 4 partner

  • Lets you be strange without translating
  • Does not need the relationship to look like other relationships
  • Reliable in private, even if unconventional in public
  • Talks about long-term plans without flinching at the foreign-feeling parts

The shadow

Number 4 can go emotionally absent when conventional pressure builds. It is not cruelty; it is overwhelm at being asked to perform a version of love they do not actually believe in.

Over the years

With a Number 4, long-term love is freer than most. Rahu rewards couples who write their own rules and resist comparing the relationship to anyone else's.

☿Number 5 · Mercury · needs conversation more than comfort

Number 5 in love is playful, curious, mentally electric, and allergic to anything that feels like a cage. Mercury loves through conversation, ideas, jokes and shared adventures, not through declarations.

Number 5 is drawn to partners who can keep up intellectually, conversationally, and in terms of movement. They want a fellow explorer, not a stationary anchor.

Green flags from a Number 5 partner

  • Texts you something interesting every day, not just check-ins
  • Plans spontaneous things and is delighted when you do too
  • Does not require routine, but shows up reliably for the things that matter
  • Talks ideas openly, including the half-formed ones

The shadow

Number 5 can chase novelty into territory that costs the relationship. It is not always infidelity; more often it is the slow drift of attention to anywhere except here.

Over the years

With a Number 5, long-term love is built on shared evolution. Couples who keep learning, moving, travelling and starting new things together last longest, because the Mercury mind needs newness even within commitment.

♀Number 6 · Venus · loves through daily acts

Number 6 in love is romantic, sensual, devoted and aesthetically present. Venus loves through beauty, comfort, the body, food, music, touch, the small daily rituals that say "you matter".

Number 6 is drawn to people who appreciate beauty in the same register: who notice details, who treat the relationship as something worth making lovely.

Green flags from a Number 6 partner

  • Creates the home you both live in, literally and emotionally
  • Remembers anniversaries, but more importantly, ordinary Tuesdays
  • Is physically affectionate without prompting
  • Treats the relationship as a creative project, made rather than just maintained

The shadow

Number 6 can confuse harmony with health. It shows up as keeping the peace at the cost of saying hard things, until the unsaid hardens into resentment.

Over the years

With a Number 6, long-term love is sensorial: the home is beautiful, the meals are good, the touch never thins out. A Venus relationship dies fastest when aesthetic care fades, so couples who keep tending the daily ritual last longest.

☋Number 7 · Ketu · needs a partner who honours silence

Number 7 in love is depth-oriented, quietly devoted, and uninterested in performance. Ketu does not flirt, it observes. When Number 7 falls, it is usually because someone showed them an inner world they could not look away from.

Number 7 is drawn to people with mystery: an undertone of solitude even in company, a private inner life that does not need to be performed. They are repelled by people who need constant validation.

Green flags from a Number 7 partner

  • Comfortable with long silences in your company
  • Asks questions most people do not think to ask
  • Does not need daily proof; absences do not threaten them
  • Shares a few real things instead of many surface things

The shadow

Number 7 can disappear into solitude when the relationship asks for ordinary intimacy. It is not coldness; it is a retreat from a closeness that started to feel demanding.

Over the years

With a Number 7, long-term love is unusually quiet. Ketu rewards couples who protect each other's solitude, who do not require constant reassurance, who can sit together without needing to fill the space.

Read further: Number 7 personality · Life Path 7 · The Inward Witness

♄Number 8 · Saturn · slow to love, stays when they do

Number 8 in love is slow, careful, deeply serious and built for the long haul. Saturn does not waste time; if they are putting time into you, they have already decided this matters.

Number 8 is drawn to people with maturity beyond their age, a sense of responsibility, and a willingness to build something durable rather than chase intensity.

Green flags from a Number 8 partner

  • Shows you their plans, finances, work, calendar, real life, not a curated version
  • Pays for things without keeping score
  • Tells you what they are afraid of, eventually
  • Treats your family with the same seriousness as their own

The shadow

Number 8 can confuse responsibility with romance. It shows up as staying in a relationship because the structure is built, even when the love has gone cold.

Over the years

With a Number 8, long-term love is rare in its solidity. Saturn rewards couples who do the unglamorous work, money conversations, family logistics, future planning, with the same attention they gave each other on the first date.

♂Number 9 · Mars · loves loud and loyal

Number 9 in love is intense, protective, passionate and not subtle. Mars loves at temperature. When Number 9 is in, you feel it; when they pull back, you feel that too.

Number 9 is drawn to courage, in a partner's work, opinions, conflicts, willingness to fight for things. They are bored by people who play it safe in their own life.

Green flags from a Number 9 partner

  • Defends you to others without being asked
  • Tells you when you are being unfair, even when it costs the moment
  • Initiates the hard conversations
  • Brings physical, kinetic energy into the relationship: sport, dance, movement, building

The shadow

Number 9 can mistake heat for love. It shows up as fighting often and calling it passion, when it is actually unprocessed anger getting routed through the relationship.

Over the years

With a Number 9, long-term love is alive. Mars rewards couples who keep the energy direct, who fight clean, who say what they mean, who do not let resentment compost. The relationship dies of suppression long before it dies of conflict.

The most useful thing on this page

Read your own section, then read your partner's. The mismatch is almost always in the "loves through" column rather than in the matrix. A Number 8 building a future and a Number 6 making the present beautiful can each feel unloved by the other while both are working hard at love, because neither is being paid in the currency they recognise. That is a translation problem, and translation problems are solvable in a way that incompatibility is not.

Frequently asked

Which number's love style applies to me?

Your Birth Number (Moolank), the single digit your day of birth reduces to. Born on the 23rd? 2+3 = 5, so read Number 5. Many people also recognise their Life Path Number's style in how they behave in longer relationships.

Does a caution pairing mean the relationship will fail?

No. Compatibility in Chaldean numerology describes the natural inclination, not the outcome. Plenty of long marriages are between pairings traditionally cautioned against; the variable is awareness. A caution pairing means the friction is predictable, which makes it easier to work with, not harder.

Why is the compatibility matrix symmetric?

Because a relationship is one thing seen from two sides. If Number 3 and Number 8 sit in different planetary groups, that is equally true read from either direction. Any compatibility table where 3 lists 8 as strong but 8 lists 3 as cautioned contains an error, since both statements describe the same pair.

Should I use Birth Number or name number for compatibility?

Birth Number for the underlying temperament, which is what this page covers. Name Number describes how you are received rather than how you operate, so it matters more for first impressions and public life than for long-term relational fit.

What if my partner and I are the same number?

Same-number pairings are strong on recognition and weak on correction. You will understand each other quickly and share the same blind spot, which means the shadow described in your section is running in both of you at once with nobody positioned to name it.
