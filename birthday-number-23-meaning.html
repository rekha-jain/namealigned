Birthday Number 23 · The Royal Star of the Lion

Versatility · Support · Success

If you were born on the 23rd of any month, your Birth Number in Chaldean numerology is 5, ruled by Mercury, the planet of communication, trade and quick thinking. Indian numerologists call this the Moolank, the root number taken from your day of birth. The 23rd is one of the most favoured birthdays in the Chaldean system because it passes through 23, the Royal Star of the Lion, a compound Cheiro associated with success, help from people in positions of influence and protection in one's plans. What does it mean to be born on the 23rd?

23 combines 2, the Moon's people sense, with 3, Jupiter's optimism and expansion, and they add to 5, Mercury. The result is a quick, curious and socially gifted personality. Mercury loves movement, ideas and exchange, and the 23rd-born usually has several interests running at once.

What sets the 23rd apart from other Mercury birthdays is its compound. Cheiro described the Royal Star of the Lion as a promise of success and of support from superiors and well-placed friends. In practice, many 23rd-born people find that the right mentor or opportunity appears when they need it, especially when they are generous with their own contacts. Compare the 5th and the 14th, which share the Mercury root.

Personality strengths of the 23rd-born

  • Quick learner who picks up new skills easily
  • Persuasive and entertaining communicator
  • Adaptable, thrives on change and travel
  • Attracts helpful allies and mentors
  • Good instinct for timing and opportunity
  • Youthful energy that lasts well into later life

Growth themes for Birthday Number 23

  • Starting many things and finishing fewer
  • Restlessness when life becomes routine
  • Saying yes to every invitation and then feeling stretched
  • Relying on charm where preparation would serve better

Mercury benefits from rhythm. A simple weekly plan and a short list of priorities lets the 23rd-born keep their variety while still finishing what matters.

The 23rd in everyday life

The 23rd-born is often the one with the latest news, the best restaurant tip and the friend in the right place. They enjoy conversation, short trips, learning languages and new technology. Their homes and phones tend to be busy, and they usually do best with work that changes from day to day. The Lion in their compound also gives a dignified, confident streak: they like to be respected for their competence, not just liked for their charm.

How the 23rd differs from the 5th and 14th

The 5th is pure Mercury, fast and free. The 14th passes through a compound of magnetic movement, so it leans toward travel and connection. The 23rd is the most supported of the three: its compound adds a sense of being backed by others, which often makes its risks pay off more reliably.

Love and compatibility for the 23rd

Mercury is friendly with 1, 3, 5, 6 and 9 in the Chaldean table. The 23rd-born needs a partner who keeps things interesting and gives them space to roam a little. Venus-ruled 6 adds warmth and home comforts, which makes Number 5 and 6 compatibility a pleasing combination. Explore love compatibility numerology for more.

Career leanings

Sales, marketing, media, journalism, travel, trading, consulting, public relations, technology and entrepreneurship. The Royal Star also favours careers with a patron or sponsor, such as roles that grow through mentorship. See Number 5 career paths.

Lucky attributes for the 23rd

The 23rd follows Mercury: Wednesday, then Friday; light green, light grey and turquoise; emerald, peridot or jade; and the dates 5, 14 and 23, with early morning and late afternoon as strong hours. See lucky attributes for Number 5, and test your name with the name numerology calculator.
