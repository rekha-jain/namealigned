How to find your house number

Add the digits of the number on your door and reduce to one digit: 247 is 2 + 4 + 7 = 13, and 1 + 3 = 4. If there is a letter, add its Chaldean value (A 1, B 2, C 3 and so on as used on this site): 12B is 1 + 2 + 2 = 5. In an apartment, numerologists usually read the flat number rather than the building number, because that is the door you live behind.

A house number works best when it supports the people in it. Compare it with your Birth Number: harmonious pairs feel easy, others simply ask for a little more conscious balance.

Frequently asked questions

How do I calculate my house number in numerology?

Add the digits on your door and reduce to a single digit. If there is a letter, add its Chaldean value. The calculator above does this automatically.

Do I use the flat number or the building number?

Most numerologists read the flat or apartment number, since that is your own front door. The building number describes the shared building.

Which house number is best?

There is no single best number. The right number depends on what you want the home to support and on your own Birth Number.

Can I change my house number energy?

Tradition suggests small adjustments such as adding a supportive number or letter beside the door. The house number pages explain options for each number.
